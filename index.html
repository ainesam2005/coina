01 — The idea

Built around a simple question:
why should sending money be so hard?

Africa is full of people, businesses and families that cross borders every day. The technology behind money should make that movement easier, not add another layer of friction.

01

Move value

Designed for borderless transfers and everyday payments without making users think about the machinery underneath.

02

Help businesses grow

Give merchants a path to accept digital payments and serve customers beyond their home market.

03

Leave room to build

APIs, SDKs and an explorer give developers a foundation for products that can sit on top of the network.
